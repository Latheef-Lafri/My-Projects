How to Use the One-Day Visit Planner

1. Adding Destinations

Click + Add to Plan on any attraction card from the home page, catalog, or attraction details page. The planner badge in the top navigation will immediately update.

2. Arranging Visit Order

Use the ▲ (Move Up) and ▼ (Move Down) arrow buttons beside each stop to reorder your itinerary to minimize travel distance or match opening hours.

3. Saving & Printing

Your plan is automatically saved in your browser's local storage. Click Print Itinerary to generate a clean, printer-friendly one-page day schedule or save as PDF.
